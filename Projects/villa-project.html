Next-Gen Villa Website

Luxury Hospitality Booking Experience

A full top-to-bottom redesign and web application for a premier luxury boutique villa in Mirissa, Sri Lanka. Engineered for elevated visual storytelling, high visitor conversion, and frictionless direct reservation flows.

Project Overview

The client approached me to completely revitalize their digital hospitality presence. The prior website suffered from dated layouts, slow load times, and an awkward reservation funnel that resulted in elevated bounce rates. The primary goal was to construct a contemporary glassmorphism-inspired digital identity that mirrors the luxurious, serene experience of staying at the property.

Beginning with comprehensive Figma wireframing, the user journey was restructured to prioritize immersive visual previews of the villa grounds, suite amenities, and private pool views. The development was completed with clean responsive web architecture, delivering lightning-fast load times, seamless typography scaling, and fluid animations across mobile and desktop devices.

Key Capabilities

Real-Time Booking Calendar

Dynamic date-range availability selector that syncs with back-office reservation availability to avoid double-bookings.

Immersive Visual Gallery

High-resolution responsive imagery showcasing villa suites, architecture, dining areas, and Mirissa surroundings.

Suite & Amenity Breakdowns

Detailed room specifications including guest capacity, bed configurations, private amenities, and bespoke concierge packages.

Mobile-First Guest Funnel

Optimized touch navigation and accelerated checkout flows designed specifically for international mobile travelers.

Technical Challenges & Solutions

Performance & High-Resolution Media

Hospitality sites demand rich photography without sacrificing page speed. Implementing modern image compression and lazy loading reduced initial payload while preserving the pristine clarity of the luxury property visuals.

Reservation Logic & Guest Inquiries

Developed responsive booking form validation with automated inquiry routing, allowing the resort team to review booking requests and communicate directly with guests.
