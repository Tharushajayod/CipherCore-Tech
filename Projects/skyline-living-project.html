Skyline Living Apartment

Apartment Sales & Management System

A full‑stack apartment sales and management platform developed as a university Software Engineering group project. The system connects property discovery, viewing appointments, reservations, payments, refunds, and ownership records through dedicated customer, staff, and administrator workflows.

Project Overview

The Skyline Living Apartment system streamlines the entire lifecycle of apartment sales – from browsing listings to final ownership registration. It supports multi‑module workflows, secure payment processing, and role‑based access for customers, staff, and administrators.

My Role & Contribution
