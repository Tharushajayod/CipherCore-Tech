D Mobile Hub

Smartphone & Accessories E-Commerce Platform

An e-commerce platform built for a phone and accessories retailer in Galle, Sri Lanka. It combines a responsive shopping experience with tools for managing products, inventory, promotions, payments, and customer orders.

Project Overview

D Mobile Hub is an end-to-end digital retail platform engineered for a phone and accessories retailer located in Galle, Sri Lanka. The project brings the full retail shopping experience into a responsive, high-performance web platform designed around regional customer preferences, device variant needs, and flexible fulfillment workflows.

The customer journey begins on the storefront catalog where shoppers browse smartphones and accessories with instant search autocomplete and fine-grained filters for brand, memory (RAM), internal storage capacity, and price range. When viewing a specific product, customers can configure options across RAM, storage, and colorways with live stock status indicators, warranty badges, and dynamic price adjustments. Shoppers can authenticate using email and password or Google sign-in, while maintaining an active shopping cart across devices.

During checkout, buyers select between in-store pickup at the Galle showroom or courier delivery across Sri Lanka. To support local purchasing habits, checkout accommodates direct bank transfer with receipt slip uploads for verification, as well as Cash on Delivery (COD) configured with an advance payment requirement to reduce delivery cancellation risks. After placing an order, customers receive downloadable PDF invoices with full warranty terms, monitor progress through a live status timeline (Order Placed, Payment Confirmed, Processing, Out for Delivery, Delivered), and leave verified-purchase reviews once received.

On the administrative side, store operators and sellers access a back-office operations suite. The management interface facilitates daily retail tasks including tracking orders, adjusting variant inventory thresholds, auditing bank payment slips, scheduling product-specific promotional coupon codes, and monitoring financial reporting metrics such as net revenue, gross profit, and fulfillment breakdowns.

Technical Focus

Variant Stock Integrity

Handling consumer electronics with multiple specifications (RAM, internal storage, colorways) requires discrete SKU stock tracking. The data model decouples parent product metadata from individual variant stock units, preventing race conditions and overselling across high-demand variants.

Checkout & Order Consistency

To support asynchronous payment methods like bank slip verification and COD advances, order progression is governed by strict state transitions. This guarantees that customer carts, invoice records, inventory reservations, and customer notification statuses remain synchronized across database operations.

Role-Based Access Control

Store administrative routes (`/admin`) and operational endpoints are strictly segregated from customer accounts. Supabase authentication combined with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) ensures that privileged operations—such as approving payment slips, adjusting stock thresholds, and configuring promotions—are restricted to verified staff.

Targeted Promotion Validation

Promotional codes are validated at the line-item level rather than as generic cart discounts. The discount logic verifies coupon eligibility against specific device models or categories, ensuring store margins remain protected during mixed-cart checkouts.
