Skyline Living Apartment

Apartment Sales & Management Web Platform

An enterprise-grade apartment sales and property management platform engineered as an academic Software Engineering group project. The system orchestrates property listings, viewing appointments, reservations, multi-stage payments, cancellations, and ownership records across dedicated customer, staff, and admin portals.

Project Overview

Skyline Living Apartment is an integrated web application designed to streamline modern residential apartment purchasing and property sales administration. In high-value real estate transactions, prospective buyers require transparent unit availability, convenient inspection appointment booking, and structured payment schedules, while property management teams require automated reservation timelines, payment verification, and verified ownership records.

The application models real-world property transactions end-to-end. Customers browse available apartment units filtered by tower, bedroom count, price, and floor level. They can schedule guided viewing appointments with sales agents, place a provisional unit reservation backed by a fee with an automatic 4-day holding timer, and choose between complete upfront payment or a phased 3-installment payment schedule.

Project Context & Engineering Contributions

Academic Context: Developed as a university Software Engineering curriculum group project modeling enterprise-level multi-module business systems.

Assigned Academic Responsibility: Payment & Transaction Management — Architected the financial engine including reservation fee verification, 4-day holding deadlines, installment schedules, cancellation policies with refund deduction calculations, and complete audit ledgers.

Six Core System Modules

The system was engineered around six interconnected functional modules supporting customer, staff, and administrative personas:

1. Apartment Listing Management

Complete property catalog with floor plans, bedroom/bathroom configurations, square footage, panoramic galleries, pricing, and live availability statuses (Available, Reserved, Sold).

2. Booking & Viewing Appointments

Customer appointment scheduler for on-site property walkthroughs with time slot validation, staff calendar synchronization, and automated confirmation workflows.

3. Reservation Management

Provisional unit locking upon reservation fee receipt. Enforces a strict 4-day countdown hold window to secure purchase rights before unit release.

4. Payment & Transaction Management

Financial ledger supporting reservation fees, full settlement, and 3-stage installment options. Handles payment proof validation, receipts, and refund processing upon cancellation.

5. Sales & Record Management

Finalized sales contract generation, buyer-to-apartment legal association, permanent deed records, and staff analytics reporting for apartment turnover.

6. Reviews, Notifications & Inquiries

Customer inquiry messaging, favorite unit bookmarking, property reviews, and real-time status notifications for appointment approvals and payment confirmations.

Deep Dive: Payment & Transaction Engine

4-Day Reservation Hold Timer & Status Transitions

When a buyer reserves a unit, a strict countdown is initiated. The system enforces state transition integrity: if remaining payments or installments are not initiated within the 4-day deadline, the unit status automatically reverts to 'Available', preventing artificial property lockouts.

Flexible Payment Plans: Full & 3-Stage Installments

Buyers can complete the purchase via full upfront settlement or an installment schedule divided across project milestones (Down payment, Intermediate build stage, and Final handover). Each installment tracks balance remaining, due dates, and payment receipts.

Cancellation Governance & Refund Management

If a buyer withdraws from a reserved or in-progress purchase, the transaction engine applies automated business logic to compute allowable refunds, deduct cancellation administrative fees, update bank payout records, and restore unit availability.

Security & Financial Audit Trail

Protected by Spring Security and JWT authentication. Every payment record, slip upload, and refund disbursement creates an immutable audit entry tied to user IDs and timestamped database rows in MS SQL Server.
